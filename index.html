About the School

A neighbourhood school with a bigger vision

Nirakar Jyoti Vidhya Public School was founded to bring quality, English-medium education within reach of families in Bajauta, Jattari and the wider Tappal block — without asking anyone to travel far from home for it.

We are a private, recognized, unaided institution teaching Grades 1 through 10, with a state-board curriculum built around strong fundamentals, discipline and genuine attention to every child.

Essential Facilities

Everything a growing child needs, on one campus

From classrooms to playgrounds, every corner of the school is built around the wellbeing and learning of your child.

Smart Classrooms

Bright, well-lit rooms with modern teaching aids for an engaging learning experience.

Library

A growing collection of books to nurture curiosity and a lifelong love of reading.

Playground & Sports

Open play areas and sports periods that keep children active, fit and team-spirited.

Computer Lab

Hands-on computer classes that build digital confidence from an early age.

Safe Transport

Reliable, supervised transport connecting nearby villages and colonies to the campus.

Safe Campus

A secure, well-monitored campus so parents can be at ease through the school day.

Experienced Faculty

Teachers who know every child by name, not just by roll number.

Co-Curricular Activities

Art, music and cultural events that help every child discover their own strengths.
